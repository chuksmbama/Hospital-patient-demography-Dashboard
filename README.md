## About the Dashbaord
The dataset includes information about:
- Patient demographics(gender, condition)
- Treatment cost
- Satisfaction ratings
- Readmission and remission status
- Outcomes (Recovered, Stable)

The analysis was performed in power BI, using DAX for calculations and visual storytelling dashbaords

## Business Questions
- How many patients were treated within the selected period?
- What is the gender distibution of patients?
- What are the most common medical condititons among patients?
- What is the average treatment cost across all patients?
- What is the distribution of patient outcomes (Recovered vs stable) ?
- How satisfied are patients on average?
- What percentage of patients required readmission?
- Are there patterns between gender, condition and treatment outcomes?


  ## KPIs
  - Total Patients: 984
  - Male Patients: 460
  - Female Patients: 524
  - Average Treatment Cost: $8,367
  - Patient Recovery Rate: 60.06%
  - Readmission Rate: 26.83%
  - Average Satisfaction Rating: 3.0
 
  ## Conclusion
  The Patient Demography Analysis Dashboard reveals that the hospital manages a balanced patient population with strong        recovery outcomes (60%) and moderate treatment costs (average $8,367). While most patients report satisfactory               experiences, a 27% readmission rate highlights the need for stronger follow-up care and preventive strategies.
  
  Overall, the analysis provides data-driven insights that can help the hospital improve patient satisfaction, reduce costs,   and enhance overall treatment outcomes.
